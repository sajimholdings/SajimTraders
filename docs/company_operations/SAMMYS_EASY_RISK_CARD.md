## 🚦 THE 4 GREEN LIGHTS (SAMMY'S 4-CHECK)

Before Alu enters a manual trade, or before any automated alert goes to customers, answer these 4 questions:

| # | Simple Question | What to Check on the Screen | Answer | Action |
|---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| **1** | **Is Market Momentum Strong?** | The Momentum number ($M$) must be **above 40** (Green). | [ ] YES &nbsp; [ ] NO | If the market is dead or below 40, do NOT enter. |
| **2** | **Is Stop-Loss Protected?** | Stop Loss must be placed behind the baseline line or behind the candle wick. | [ ] YES &nbsp; [ ] NO | Never trade without an exit shield. |
| **3** | **Is Lot Size Safe (0.01)?** | Lot size must be strictly **0.01** on our small accounts. | [ ] YES &nbsp; [ ] NO | No gambling, no oversize lots. 0.01 protects the balance. |
| **4** | **Is There High-Impact News?** | No major red news (US CPI, NFP, Fed interest rates) right now. | [ ] YES &nbsp; [ ] NO | If major news is dropping, wait 15 minutes for the market to calm down. |

---

## 🛑 YOUR DECISION (SAMMY'S CALL)

* **If all 4 are YES:** Sammy says $\rightarrow$ **"GO"** (Approve the trade).
* **If even 1 is NO:** Sammy says $\rightarrow$ **"STOP"** (Reject the trade, wait for a cleaner setup).

## 🛡️ DAILY EMERGENCY BRAKE (DAILY LOSS LIMIT)
- If the account loses **$2.00 to $3.00** in a single day (or 3.5% on an FTMO challenge):
  - **Stop all trading for the rest of the day immediately.**
  - Close the charts until the next morning.
